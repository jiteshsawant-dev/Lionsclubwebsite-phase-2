Our global family

Lions International.
1.4 million strong.

Lions Clubs International was founded by Melvin Jones in Chicago in 1917. With the chartering of Windsor Ontario Lions Club in 1920, the association became international. Melvin Jones was conferred the title of Secretary General of Lions International in 1950, and his birthday on 13th January is celebrated as Founders Day with January observed as a Rededication Month.

Lionism was introduced in India in 1956 by Lion Noshir Pundole with the organisation of Lions Club of Bombay Host on 3rd February. Today, India boasts nearly 8,500 clubs and the second-largest membership in the world, with close to 290,000 members.

We belong to the ISAAME Constitution Area (India, South Asia, Africa and the Middle East) one of 7 constitution areas worldwide.

Mumbai & our district

India comprises 7 Multiple Districts. We are part of Multiple District 323 West, covering Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh and the Union Territories of Daman & Diu, Dadra and Nagar Haveli home to 16 districts, 1,343 clubs and 53,263 members.

Mumbai has 3 districts: 323 A1, A2 & A3 with 250 clubs and 13,000+ members. District 323 A1, our home district, has 61 clubs with 3,360 members.
