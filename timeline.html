Our Journey
Era 1 · Roots & founding · 1977

Ten Lions. One Dream. A Club That Changed Mumbai.

A small but determined band of Lions broke away from Lions Club of Bombay Central to plant a new seed in the heart of Mumbai. They were ten in number: Lion Maneck Dastur, Lion Kersi Commissariat, Lion Pervez Buhariwalla, Lion Homi Divwala, Lion Phiroze Javeri, Lion Soli Kutar, Lion Cowas Palia, Lion Dorab Tata, Lion Shabbir Babrawalla, and Lion Kaizer Vasi. At just 29, Lion Maneck Dastur became the youngest Charter President of any Lions Club in the world. Their founding promise: fellowship, service, and able administration, still guides every Lion who wears this badge today.

Era 1 · Roots & founding · 1977–78

The first roar — Drug Bank launched.

In its very first year, the Club launched the Drug Bank, a brainchild of Lion Baji Nariman Maloo, supported by Late Lion Dr. Rustom Hakim. Within months it was operating with the efficiency of a nationalised bank, providing medicines to those who could not afford them. The habit of winning was also formed in this first year.

Era 1 · Roots & founding · 1979–80

Saving lives at the Ganpati immersion.

President Late Lion Darius Gotla donated a Water Safety Patrol project to the Mumbai Police, a gesture that has been saving lives at the Ganpati immersion ceremony every year since.

Public safetyCommunity impact
Era 2 · Growing deeper · 1980–81

The warmth of jute on a winter night.

Lions members went out after dinner meetings into the streets of Byculla in December and January, quietly covering pavement dwellers with raw jute sheets. No cameras. No press. Just pure service. This remains one of the most remembered acts of humanness in the Club's history.

Rebuilding lives after Latur.

When the devastating earthquake struck Latur, the Club didn't just donate â€” it built. Thirteen tenements were constructed at Lions Village in Kawtha for earthquake victims. ₹7,80,000 was spent in service that year alone.

Era 2 · Growing deeper · 1994–95

Best president. Best secretary. Best docket.

Under President Lion Jimmy Firoze Khan, the Club swept three of the district's top honours in a single year. The Club adopted 16 children at the Happy Home and School for the Blind and sponsored open heart surgery for a patient in need.

Era 2 · Growing deeper · 1995–96

History written at the multiple.

The Club won the prestigious Late Lion Minoo J.D. Engineer Trophy for Best Secretary of the Multiple District and also won the Lions Quiz at the Multiple level. ₹9 Lakhs spent on service projects in one year.

Era 3 · Scaling impact · 2002–03

Best club in the district.

President Lion Aspi Cooper led the Club to win the Best President Award and Best Administered Club Award, completing ₹7 Lakhs in service projects covering eye care, education for the deaf, and support for the blind.

Best club awardEye careEducation
Era 3 · Scaling impact · 2003–04

₹15.50 lakhs — a club record.

President Lion Eruch Dossabhoy Mistry oversaw the highest service project expenditure in the Club's history at that time. The landmark achievement: handing over an Outreach Mobile Van, funded by Lions Club International Foundation's first-ever grant to the Club, to B.Y.L. Nair Hospital, valued at ₹8.62 Lakhs.

LCIF grantMobile healthcareMilestone
Era 3 · Scaling impact · 2004–05

From Mumbai to Chennai — serving tsunami victims.

When the 2004 tsunami devastated the coast, President Lion Kobad Doodhmal led a team to Chennai to distribute essential items, twinning with Lions Club of Chennai. The Club also donated a generator to B.Y.L. Nair Hospital.

Tsunami reliefNational outreach
Era 4 · Deepening legacy · 2013–14

Vision and childhood cancer become our calling.

The Club made vision and childhood cancer its twin pillars of priority. Probe accessories, Rose Trial Lens, ophthalmic instruments, and medical equipment worth lakhs were donated to hospitals, led by Project Champions Lion Sorab Bulsara and Lion Percy Kaikobad.

Childhood cancerVisionBYL Nair Hospital
Era 4 · Deepening legacy · 2023–24

₹46 lakhs in service. 261 units of blood. 90 members strong.

President Lion Adil C. Bhesadia, PMJF, led a transformational year. Projects worth ₹46 Lakhs were executed across Mumbai, Navsari, and Nashik. A mega project of ₹27 Lakhs at Dharampur's Shrimad Rajchandra Hospital benefitted thousands of premature Adivasi babies. Membership grew from 70 to 90 Lions.

Record serviceBlood donationAdivasi healthcare
Era 4 · Deepening legacy · 2024–25

Say less, do more.

Under President Lion Adil Mistry, the Club entered its most purposeful year yet, with nearly 95 members and a focus on childhood cancer, diabetes, hunger, education, and animal welfare. A "Do-Good Cookbook" was created by Lion Ladies, with proceeds directed to paediatric cancer and epilepsy care.

95 membersChildhood cancerDo-Good Cookbook
Golden Jubilee Milestone · 2027

50 years of service. One unbroken promise.

As the Lions Club of Byculla enters its Golden Jubilee year, nearly 100 Lions stand together, carrying the same spirit of fellowship, service, and able administration that Lion Maneck Dastur and 8 founding members ignited in 1977. The journey continues. The promise remains.
