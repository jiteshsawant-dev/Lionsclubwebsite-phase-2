Our method

How service actually happens.

Behind every camp, every surgery, every scholarship is a discipline refined over 50 years.
Five steps. No shortcuts. Every rupee accounted for and photographically documented.

Appeal

Individuals or institutions submit requests for assistance through the club's service channels.

Review & approve

Applications are evaluated by the Board and approved based on need, urgency, and impact.

Organise funds

Member contributions, fundraising initiatives, and CSR partnerships help secure the required support.

Deliver

Approved aid is distributed through Lions volunteers and partner organizations.

Document

Every project is photographed, documented, and uploaded to the Lions portal for transparency and accountability.

What began with 8 Lions and a dream in 1977 has grown into one of Mumbai’s most active service institutions. Inaugurated at the Taj Mahal Hotel, we have never stopped. Every year, more projects, more reach, more lives touched.
