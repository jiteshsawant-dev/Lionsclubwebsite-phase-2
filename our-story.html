How it all began.

Eight lions, a plan sketched out on the CCI lawns, and a charter received on 30th July 1977 — the founding of the Lions Club of Byculla.

Our history

How it all began.

Some years ago, a few members of the Lions Club of Bombay Central decided to branch out on their own and form a new club Lions Club of Byculla.

It was a band of just 8 to 10 lions led by the young and dashing Lion Maneck Dastur, while the others included Kersi Commissariat, Byram Kanga, Darius Gotla, Homi Tavadia, Paeterasp Patel, J.J. Rodriguez, Dhunji Hodiwala and Dinshaw Minocher.

One of their first meetings was at the C.C.I. lawns, where they chalked out the plan of the new club. As they planned to concentrate the membership drive in and around Rustom Baug, they thought it appropriate to name it “Lions Club of Byculla”. Though Darius, Homi and Byram were much older than Maneck, everyone agreed that he should be the Charter President and he created history by becoming the youngest Charter President of any Lions Club in the world.

In those days, Maneck Dastur was the voice of Lions Club of Byculla. He played a key role in setting it up, but behind him was the support of the lions mentioned above, as well as the lion-hearted Lion Baji Nariman Maloo.

The club was built on 3 key concepts: fellowship, service and able administration. Although they managed to keep their heads above water, they struggled at times as most charter members were from the core middle class, white-collar professionals. None were born with a golden spoon, so the club depended more on generosity of heart than generosity of purse.

The club was inaugurated at its first charter night by Police Commissioner of Mumbai, Mr. V.V. Chaubal, at the Taj Mahal Hotel. The Charter was received on 30th July 1977 at a glittering function at the hands of PDG Lion Dr. Harpal. The Police Commissioner was invited to join as an Honorary Member.

None of us was born with a golden spoon in our mouth so we had to depend more on the generosity of heart and not of the purse.

From the founding chronicles
Our founding mission

Three pillars, one purpose.

From the very first meeting at the CCI lawns, the Lions Club of Byculla was built on three concepts that continue to guide every member and every project to this day.

01

Service

From the very first project distributing food at Chowpatty to crores worth of medical donations, service has always been the measure of a Lions year. Not the size of the purse, but the size of the effort.

02

Fellowship

Bonds forged between members who shared more than a badge. The club was built by ordinary people with extraordinary generosity of heart, who showed up for each other as much as for the community.

03

Able Administration

A club that runs well serves better. Rigorous dockets, award-winning secretaries, and consistent recognition at district and multiple-district level reflect a founding commitment to excellence in governance.
