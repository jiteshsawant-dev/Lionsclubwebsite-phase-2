MJF Lion Daara B. Patel

District Governor, District 323 A1
2008 – 2009

MJF Lion Daara B. Patel joined Lionism in 1979 and became one of the most respected leaders in District 323 A1. As District Governor, he led under the vision “Service Always in All Ways,” expanding community impact through innovative service initiatives.

Beyond Lionism, he has held senior leadership positions across the pharmaceutical and aviation industries and currently serves as Secretary-General of the Indian Drug Manufacturers’ Association (IDMA).

  • Led the first International Sight First Twinning Project, benefiting 4,000+ patients and distributing 3,000 free spectacles.
  • Introduced “Service Sunday,” later adopted across Multiple Districts in India.
  • Organized the first joint MERL Seminar for Districts 323 A1, A2 & A3.
  • Recipient of multiple International President’s Appreciation Certificates and prestigious leadership awards.
  • Served as Faculty Member at the Lions Clubs International Training Institute and delivered lectures across Asia and the USA.
  • Featured in the International Edition of Who’s Who of the Asian Pacific Rim for professional excellence.
