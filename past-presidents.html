Continued the club’s tradition of exemplary district-level administration and service.

Lion Burges Hoshang Wania

Past President of Leo Club of Queensway before rising through Lions leadership ranks.

2010 – 11
Lion Pervez Kanga

Joined Lionism in 2002–03. Retired from Larsen & Toubro after 32 years. Guided by senior members to lead with distinction.

2009 – 10
Lion Tehmasp Dorabshaw Munshi
MJF · Best club award

Secured FCRA registration from Govt. of India. Completed Nair Hospital ophthalmic mega-project. ₹14+ Lakhs.

2008 – 09
Lion Percy Kaikobad
MJF · Best president, region

LCIF grant of US$75,000 for Nair Hospital ophthalmic ward upgrade. Projects ₹14.5 Lakhs.

2007 – 08
Lion Noshir Jamshedji Divecha
MJF

International twinning eye camp (California & Nevada) 2,000+ examined, spectacles donated. Projects ₹17 Lakhs.

2006 – 07
Lion Rohinton D. Panthaki

Projects worth ₹14 Lakhs including twinning projects, eye camps & blood donation drives.

2005 – 06
Lion Soli Pestonji Tavadia

Projects worth ₹6 Lakhs. Distributed essential items to flood victims at Ambernath, Badiapur and remote areas.

2004 – 05
Lion Kobad Burjorji Doodhmal

Donated a generator to Nair Hospital. Led team to Chennai post-tsunami relief with Lions Club of Chennai.

2003 – 04
Lion Eruch Dossabhoy Mistry

Record projects worth ₹15.5 Lakhs. Handed over Outreach Mobile Van (LCIF grant ₹8.62 Lakhs) to Nair Hospital.

2002 – 03
Lion Aspi Cooper
MJF · Best president, MD 323

Won Best President & Best Administered Club awards. Won Late Minoo J.D. Engineer Trophy a record 3rd time for LCB.

2001 – 02
Lion Rupesh Jassani
110 projects · 52 weeks

Record 110 projects in 52 weeks. 40 medical camps, 1,500 spectacles, 13,000 lives touched. Most Outstanding Club Award.

2001 – 02
Late Lion Dhunjishaw F. Ichhaporia
In memoriam · 1–8 July 2001

Our 25th President, who served for just seven days before his sudden passing remembered for his perfectionism and dedication.

2000 – 01
Late Lion Piloo Gustadji Kaka
In memoriam

Donated vitrectomy equipment for eye surgeries. Personally led relief mission to Bhuj earthquake villages.

1999 – 2000
Lion Aspi Minocher
Best secretary, multiple district

Renovation of Operation Theatre & construction of polyclinic cabins at Nagpada Neighbourhood House.

1998 – 99
Lion Shahrukh Ratanshaw Marolia

Pioneered a common platform at district level for Vice District Governor aspirants. Charity ball at the Yacht Club.

1997 – 98
Lion Phiroze Bomanji Javeri

Donated group speech training equipment to School for the Deaf and a Vacuum Suction Unit to Nair Hospital.

1996 – 97
Late Lion Maneck Tata
In memoriam

Twinning project with Lions Club of Lonavala & Bombay Queensway. Sponsored telephone booth for a physically handicapped person.

1995 – 96
Lion Ali Akbar Changi

Won Late Lion Minoo J.D. Engineer Trophy (Best Secretary, Multiple District). Projects worth ₹9 Lakhs.

1994 – 95
Lion Jimmy Firoze Khan

Won Best President, Best Secretary & Best Docket awards. Sponsored an open heart surgery.

1993 – 94
Lion Percy Ratanshaw Buhariwala
MJF

Built 13 tenements at Lions Village in Kawtha for Latur earthquake victims. Total projects: ₹7.8 Lakhs.

1992 – 93
Lion Prakash Laxmanrao Tate
MJF

Renovated library at Sir J.J. School & donated a cloth-cutting machine to National Job Development Centre.

1991 – 92
Lion Neville Behramshaw Mehta

Donated an imported wheelchair; organised medical & eye check-up camps at Saphala Village.

1990 – 91
Lion Shabbir H. Babrawala

First Burns Unit in Maharashtra at Masina Hospital (9 beds, ₹1 Lakh). Sponsored baby cancer treatment in the USA.

1989 – 90
Lion Cawas Darabshaw Palia
MJF

Built a recreation room for the National Association for the Blind at Ambernath.

1988 – 89
Lion Adi Maneksha Mistry

Soundproofed the Language Laboratory at Kamla Mehta School for the Blind, Dadar.

1987 – 88
Lion Hoshang Nadirshaw Wania
Best secretary award

Donated dental equipment to Masina Hospital. Organised blood drives & sponsored Police trophies.

1986 – 87
Late Lion Mohammed Zoyeb Merchant
In memoriam

The “Finance Minister” of the club. Donated equipment to Nair Hospital & Savera School.

1985 – 86
Lion Parvez Framroz Buhariwalla

Built rooftop garden at Asha Sadan Child Care Centre, inaugurated by Field Marshall Sam Maneckshaw.

1983 – 84
Lion Homi Sorabji Divwala

Soundproofed & air-conditioned classrooms at Hellen Keller Institute for the deaf and mute.

1981 – 82
Lion Kersi K. Commissariat

Electrification of 25 Adivasi huts at Talasari, Gujarat. Sponsored Lioness Club of Byculla.

1980 – 81
Late Lion Baji Nariman Maloo
MJF In memoriam

Originator of the Drug Bank & Night of Nights. Health fair benefitting 5,000 people.

1979 – 80
Late Lion Darius Gotla
In memoriam

Donated the Water Safety Patrol project to Mumbai Police for Ganpati immersions.

1978 – 79
Late Lion Homi P. Tavadia
In memoriam

104 service projects in a single year. Championed small, impactful community projects.

1977 – 78 · Charter
Lion Maneck Homi Dastur
Charter president

Youngest Charter President in the world, aged 29. Inaugurated the Lions Drug Bank.
