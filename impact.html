Every life we touch
is a life we've changed.

For nearly 50 years, the Lions Club of Byculla has shown up, for the sick, the hungry, the blind, the orphaned, the elderly, and the forgotten, with one unwavering conviction: that service is not charity, it is dignity.

Service in action

This is what "say less, do more" looks like in real life.

Four stories from five decades of service. Each one ordinary to us, life-changing to someone else.

The premature babies of Gujarat

In 2023–24, Lions Club of Byculla undertook a landmark Rs. 27 Lakh project at Shrimad Rajchandra Hospital in Dharampur, Gujarat, providing critical support for thousands of premature Adivasi newborns who would otherwise have had little access to specialised neonatal care. This single project stands as one of the most significant humanitarian interventions in our club's history.

The blood donation at CST

At one of Mumbai's busiest railway stations, we set up blood donation camps that collected over 261 units of blood, reaching commuters, travellers, and everyday citizens, and showing that service doesn't need a stage.

The do-good cookbook

When our Lion Ladies wanted to make a difference, they wrote a cookbook. Every recipe was given by a member with love. Every book was offered as a gift in exchange for a donation. Every rupee raised goes toward paediatric cancer and epilepsy treatment. This is what community-driven impact looks like.

The jute sheets of Byculla

In our earliest years, after a club dinner meeting, our members, led by past presidents and Lion Ladies, would walk through the streets of Byculla late at night and cover sleeping pavement-dwellers with raw jute sheets during winter. No press. No recognition. Just warmth, offered quietly in the dark.
