Nearly fifty years of the Lions Club of Byculla — our story, the journey, the people who lead it, and the movement we belong to.

Our history

How it all began.

Some years ago, a few members of the Lions Club of Bombay Central decided to branch out on their own and form a new club Lions Club of Byculla.

It was a band of just 8 to 10 lions led by the young and dashing Lion Maneck Dastur, while the others included Kersi Commissariat, Byram Kanga, Darius Gotla, Homi Tavadia, Paeterasp Patel, J.J. Rodriguez, Dhunji Hodiwala and Dinshaw Minocher.

One of their first meetings was at the C.C.I. lawns, where they chalked out the plan of the new club. As they planned to concentrate the membership drive in and around Rustom Baug, they thought it appropriate to name it “Lions Club of Byculla”. Though Darius, Homi and Byram were much older than Maneck, everyone agreed that he should be the Charter President and he created history by becoming the youngest Charter President of any Lions Club in the world.

In those days, Maneck Dastur was the voice of Lions Club of Byculla. He played a key role in setting it up, but behind him was the support of the lions mentioned above, as well as the lion-hearted Lion Baji Nariman Maloo.

The club was built on 3 key concepts: fellowship, service and able administration. Although they managed to keep their heads above water, they struggled at times as most charter members were from the core middle class, white-collar professionals. None were born with a golden spoon, so the club depended more on generosity of heart than generosity of purse.

The club was inaugurated at its first charter night by Police Commissioner of Mumbai, Mr. V.V. Chaubal, at the Taj Mahal Hotel. The Charter was received on 30th July 1977 at a glittering function at the hands of PDG Lion Dr. Harpal. The Police Commissioner was invited to join as an Honorary Member.

None of us was born with a golden spoon in our mouth so we had to depend more on the generosity of heart and not of the purse.

From the founding chronicles
Our founding mission

Three pillars, one purpose.

From the very first meeting at the CCI lawns, the Lions Club of Byculla was built on three concepts that continue to guide every member and every project to this day.

01

Service

From the very first project distributing food at Chowpatty to crores worth of medical donations, service has always been the measure of a Lions year. Not the size of the purse, but the size of the effort.

02

Fellowship

Bonds forged between members who shared more than a badge. The club was built by ordinary people with extraordinary generosity of heart, who showed up for each other as much as for the community.

03

Able Administration

A club that runs well serves better. Rigorous dockets, award-winning secretaries, and consistent recognition at district and multiple-district level reflect a founding commitment to excellence in governance.

Era 1 · Roots & founding · 1977

Ten Lions. One Dream. A Club That Changed Mumbai.

A small but determined band of Lions broke away from Lions Club of Bombay Central to plant a new seed in the heart of Mumbai. They were ten in number: Lion Maneck Dastur, Lion Kersi Commissariat, Lion Pervez Buhariwalla, Lion Homi Divwala, Lion Phiroze Javeri, Lion Soli Kutar, Lion Cowas Palia, Lion Dorab Tata, Lion Shabbir Babrawalla, and Lion Kaizer Vasi. At just 29, Lion Maneck Dastur became the youngest Charter President of any Lions Club in the world. Their founding promise: fellowship, service, and able administration, still guides every Lion who wears this badge today.

Era 1 · Roots & founding · 1977–78

The first roar — Drug Bank launched.

In its very first year, the Club launched the Drug Bank, a brainchild of Lion Baji Nariman Maloo, supported by Late Lion Dr. Rustom Hakim. Within months it was operating with the efficiency of a nationalised bank, providing medicines to those who could not afford them. The habit of winning was also formed in this first year.

Era 2 · Growing deeper · 1980–81

The warmth of jute on a winter night.

Lions members went out after dinner meetings into the streets of Byculla in December and January, quietly covering pavement dwellers with raw jute sheets. No cameras. No press. Just pure service. This remains one of the most remembered acts of humanness in the Club's history.

Our pride

District Governor,
2008 – 2009.

MJF Lion Daara B. Patel

District Governor, District 323 A1
2008 – 2009

MJF Lion Daara B. Patel joined Lionism in 1979 and became one of the most respected leaders in District 323 A1. As District Governor, he led under the vision “Service Always in All Ways,” expanding community impact through innovative service initiatives.

Beyond Lionism, he has held senior leadership positions across the pharmaceutical and aviation industries and currently serves as Secretary-General of the Indian Drug Manufacturers’ Association (IDMA).

  • Led the first International Sight First Twinning Project, benefiting 4,000+ patients and distributing 3,000 free spectacles.
  • Introduced “Service Sunday,” later adopted across Multiple Districts in India.
  • Organized the first joint MERL Seminar for Districts 323 A1, A2 & A3.
  • Recipient of multiple International President’s Appreciation Certificates and prestigious leadership awards.
  • Served as Faculty Member at the Lions Clubs International Training Institute and delivered lectures across Asia and the USA.
  • Featured in the International Edition of Who’s Who of the Asian Pacific Rim for professional excellence.

Our global family

Lions International.
1.4 million strong.

Lions Clubs International was founded by Melvin Jones in Chicago in 1917. With the chartering of Windsor Ontario Lions Club in 1920, the association became international. Melvin Jones was conferred the title of Secretary General of Lions International in 1950, and his birthday on 13th January is celebrated as Founders Day with January observed as a Rededication Month.

Lionism was introduced in India in 1956 by Lion Noshir Pundole with the organisation of Lions Club of Bombay Host on 3rd February. Today, India boasts nearly 8,500 clubs and the second-largest membership in the world, with close to 290,000 members.

We belong to the ISAAME Constitution Area (India, South Asia, Africa and the Middle East) one of 7 constitution areas worldwide.

Mumbai & our district

India comprises 7 Multiple Districts. We are part of Multiple District 323 West, covering Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh and the Union Territories of Daman & Diu, Dadra and Nagar Haveli home to 16 districts, 1,343 clubs and 53,263 members.

Mumbai has 3 districts: 323 A1, A2 & A3 with 250 clubs and 13,000+ members. District 323 A1, our home district, has 61 clubs with 3,360 members.
